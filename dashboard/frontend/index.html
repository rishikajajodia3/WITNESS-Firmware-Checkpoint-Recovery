Scope & provenance

Architecture

WITNESS is a firmware/architecture proposal validated through a discrete-event software prototype, not a claim about existing NVMe-MI or FDP semantics, and not a claim of running on real SSD hardware.

Technical Flow
WITNESS: a software simulation of a proposed SSD firmware feature. No real NAND, SMBus, or NVMe-MI wire protocol is touched anywhere in this dashboard or its backend.
